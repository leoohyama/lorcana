True Value

What the card is really worth today, reconciled from every live price signal — eBay raw & graded asks and the JustTCG market — into one figure. Grading premiums are divided back out and inflated asks discounted, so it reads as a realized, raw-card price.

Fair Value

What the card should be worth given only its own characteristics — art, stats, rarity, set, demand and character fame — learned from every other card. It ignores the card's own price, so it's an independent second opinion.

Mispricing

True ÷ Fair − 1. Positive (red) = the market pays above what the card's traits justify; negative (blue) = it trades below. A read on dislocation, not a guaranteed trade.

Experimental — a research view of a latent-pricing model, not investment advice. "Fair value" is a statistical estimate with real uncertainty; treat large gaps as questions to investigate, not signals to act on. Static snapshot as of … (the main dashboard is live).
